# Code of Conduct

SquareStar welcomes constructive contributions from people with different levels of
experience and different technical opinions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, design, bugs, and tradeoffs.
- Critique the change or idea, not the person.
- Give contributors room to ask basic questions and learn the project.
- Prefer reproducible evidence, tests, benchmarks, and clear reasoning over status or
  authority.
- Keep reviews focused on the pull request; avoid unrelated demands that unnecessarily
  expand a contributor's scope.
- Do not post private information, credentials, security-sensitive exploit details, or
  third-party data that should not be redistributed.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate humiliation, sexualized
attention, doxxing, or sustained bad-faith disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation
when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reviews and disagreements

Technical disagreement is normal. When reasonable approaches differ, explain the
constraint you are optimizing for — correctness, latency, maintainability, platform
behavior, compatibility, or scope — and use tests/measurements where possible.

A contributor should not need to understand the whole application to make a focused
change. Reviews should help preserve boundaries rather than punish unfamiliarity.

## Security reports

Do not disclose a vulnerability publicly before maintainers have had a reasonable
chance to assess it. Follow `SECURITY.md` for security-reporting guidance.
